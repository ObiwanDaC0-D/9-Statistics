PROBABILITY LAB

Watch the law of large numbers

Simulate independent Bernoulli trials with a selected probability of success. The running proportion should tend toward the true probability as trials accumulate, though not monotonically.

PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTIONS · V2

Explore five probability models

Choose a distribution, adjust its parameters, and explore its probability density or mass function. Shaded bars or curves represent a selected probability interval. All calculations are local, with numerical approximations for continuous probabilities.

SAMPLING DISTRIBUTIONS

See the central limit theorem

Draw independent samples from a strongly right-skewed exponential population (mean = 10, SD = 10). Observe how the distribution of sample means changes with sample size.

INFERENTIAL STATISTICS

Confidence intervals and hypothesis tests

One-sample z procedures assuming independent observations and a known population standard deviation. The displayed two-sided p-value tests H₀: μ = μ₀.

REGRESSION LAB

Fit and interpret a linear relationship

Simple ordinary least squares regression with an intercept. Generate synthetic observations with adjustable slope and noise, or analyze two numeric columns from the Data Studio.

DATA STUDIO

Explore your own CSV data

Import a CSV locally (maximum 2 MB, 10,000 rows, 60 columns). The file is not uploaded. Select numeric columns for summary statistics, correlation, and regression.
