Watch the law of large numbers

Simulate independent Bernoulli trials with a selected probability of success. The running proportion should tend toward the true probability as trials accumulate, though not monotonically.

SAMPLING DISTRIBUTIONS

See the central limit theorem

Draw independent samples from a strongly right-skewed exponential population (mean = 10, SD = 10). Observe how the distribution of sample means changes with sample size.

INFERENTIAL STATISTICS

Confidence intervals and hypothesis tests

One-sample z procedures assuming independent observations and a known population standard deviation. The displayed two-sided p-value tests H₀: μ = μ₀.

HYPOTHESIS TESTING · V3

Statistical tests, effect sizes & power

Choose a test, inspect assumptions, and calculate test statistics and p-values. These educational tools use analytic approximations and synthetic or manually entered summary data; they do not establish causal effects.

STATISTICAL POWER

Explore power and sample size

Approximate two-sided independent-groups z-test power with equal sample sizes, common SD, and normal outcomes. This is an approximation to—not an exact calculation for—a two-sample t-test.

REGRESSION LAB

Fit and interpret a linear relationship

Simple ordinary least squares regression with an intercept. Generate synthetic observations with adjustable slope and noise, or analyze two numeric columns from the Data Studio.

MULTIVARIATE STATISTICS · V4

Model relationships among multiple variables

Fit ordinary least squares or binary logistic regression to synthetic or imported numeric CSV data. Compare adjusted coefficients, examine interaction terms, and inspect collinearity and residuals. Observational associations are not necessarily causal.

MULTICOLLINEARITY

Variance inflation factors

VIF = 1 / (1 − R²) from auxiliary predictor regressions. High VIF can increase coefficient uncertainty; thresholds are context-dependent. With an interaction, VIFs may be sensitive to variable centering.
